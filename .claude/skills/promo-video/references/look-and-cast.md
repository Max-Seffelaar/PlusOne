## 1. Look bible

One Saturday night at Kelder Nord, a fictional underground club in a raw concrete building in Amsterdam. The app itself (`design-system.md`) is near-black `#0B0B0D` with one lavender accent `#B5A6FF`; the video carries the same identity.

| Element | Choice |
|---|---|
| Light | Hard direct on-camera flash, crisp highlights on leather and wet pavement, deep black falloff behind the subject. In video: "looks like on-camera flash photography" — hard frontal light, black behind. |
| Colour | Blacks and greys; lavender neon (`#B5A6FF`) is the only colour. No other neon. |
| Place | Raw concrete walls, black steel doors, wet street, a plain steel crowd barrier (never velvet rope or brass stanchions). Inside: long black bar under curved lavender neon lines, haze. |
| Crowd | Twenties and thirties, more than half women, ethnically diverse, every face and outfit different (bobs, curls, braids, buzz cuts, long hair; leather, wool coats, knitwear, denim, corduroy). Stylish but wearable. Talking and laughing; at most one or two on a phone. Not fetish (no harnesses, mesh tops, bare chests), not bottle-service mainstream, not all clones. |
| Never | Text, logos, signage, readable bottle labels, wristbands, smoking, vapor, paper guest lists (unless it is the deliberate "before"), brand logos on devices, anyone who could read as under 21. |
| Camera | Real lenses and moves with a reason (push-in toward the decision maker, follow, over-the-shoulder). One continuous take per shot. |
| Overlays | Bricolage Grotesque headlines, Hanken Grotesk small text, white with one lavender word. English, tone of voice per `tone-of-voice.md`. |
| Music | Dark minimal house/techno around 122 BPM; cuts on the beat. Generations are always made with sound off. |

Reusable blocks for every video prompt (see `prompt-format.md`):

```
[General style & framing] Photorealistic, like candid nightlife flash photography brought to life: hard frontal light as if from an on-camera flash, crisp highlights on leather and wet pavement, deep black falloff behind the subject, a single lavender neon accent (#B5A6FF) as the only colour in a palette of blacks and greys, subtle 35mm film grain, natural skin texture, real human weight and timing. No text, no logos, no signage, no smoking, no vapor. Nobody looks into the lens.
```

Negative prompt, where a model has the field: `text, letters, subtitles, logos, watermark, warped screen, extra fingers, distorted hands, flicker, soft beauty lighting, bokeh portrait, retouched skin, smoking, vapor`.

## 4. Building a character in Higgsfield

| Where | For | How |
|---|---|---|
| Soul Character (Soul ID) | Stills and startframes with the same face | Train on the REF + the sheet; generate with Soul Cinema and that character |
| Element (`@Name`) | Video: identity held while moving | Element with the sheet images; `@Name` in the video prompt |

The AI Influencer / Marketing Studio avatars are for talking-to-camera UGC ads — not for this film.

Edits of an approved scene (e.g. turning two guests toward Lotte) are done with Nano Banana Pro as an edit of that image, not with Soul: an edit keeps the rest of the scene intact.
