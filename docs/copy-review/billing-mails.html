Final copy after Max's picks: one version per mail, the help line decided. Copy only, nothing built yet.

Every variant passed the humanizer check (no em dashes, no AI vocabulary). Voice: tone-of-voice.md.

Decisions (Max, 2026-10-09)

  1. Final picks: trial-0 B, trial-7 C, trial-12 A, trial-ended A, trial-21 C, payment-failed A, canceled A. Help line B.
  2. Billing mails are replyable. Every footer ends with "Reply to this email with any question. It goes straight to the PlusOne team."; replies go to support@plus-one.io (reply-to).
  3. Days 7, 12, 14 and 21 count from the trial end date, so a trial override moves them along.
  4. Day 21 is sent.
  5. payment-failed and canceled go out every time, keyed per Stripe event or invoice, not once per company.
  6. A short trial override skips any mail whose day has already passed. No catch-up.
  7. No amounts anywhere; prices live in Stripe. No dynamic counts in billing mails.

Sender

  • From: PlusOne <support@plus-one.io>, Reply-to: support@plus-one.io. A visible "noreply@" next to "Reply to this email" reads as a contradiction. If the sender stays noreply@, reply-to still routes replies to support@ and the copy holds.
