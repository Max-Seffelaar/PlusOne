Billing mails: copy review

Seven mails PlusOne sends to company admins and finance about the trial and payments (ClickUp Billing-mails B1, z8uq9m2z19). Copy only: nothing here is built yet. Each mail has three variants; the recommended one has a lavender border.

Every variant passed the humanizer check (no em dashes, no AI vocabulary). Voice: tone-of-voice.md, method: docs/copy-prompt.md (A plain, B house voice, C different structure).

How to choose

Reply with one letter per id, for example trial-7: B, payment-failed: A. Mix freely ("trial-12: A with the subject of C"). No reply on an id means the recommended pick.

Shared rules in every billing mail

  • From PlusOne <noreply@plus-one.io>. To: admins and finance of the company. Never to Always free (comped) companies, never when billing mails are paused.
  • No amounts and no price ids. Prices live in Stripe. Stripe sends invoices and payment confirmations itself; we never do.
  • Placeholders: {first_name} (no profile name: "Hi,"), {company}, {trial_end_date} (any date, so longer trials work), {cta_url} (Billing in the web app; the native app only claims auth links, so the button always opens the browser).
  • The soft block, stated the same way everywhere: new events, team invites, and imports pause; the door, planned events, their guest lists, and all data keep working.
  • Structure follows the team mails in src/features/mail/templates.ts: wordmark, heading, body, one button, fallback link, footer with support@plus-one.io.

Schedule

Day numbers are for a standard 14-day trial. Every mail after day 0 is counted from the trial end date, so a trial override moves them along (ClickUp: "override +30 days: the day-12 mail comes 2 days before the new end date"). Trial mails stop as soon as the subscription is active.

Open questions for Max

  1. Day 21: send or skip? Recommendation: send, as variant C (ask what was missing). It's the last one either way.
  2. Days counted from the trial end (not from the start) for 7, 12, 14 and 21. That's how the copy is written ("ends in 7 days"). Confirm.
  3. payment-failed once or every time? The ClickUp task makes mail_log unique on (company, type). For payment-failed and canceled that would mean one mail per company, ever. Suggest keying those two on the Stripe invoice or event id instead.
  4. Stripe dunning end state. payment-failed C says "if the payment keeps failing, the subscription ends". True only if dunning ends in "cancel subscription". If you want A or B, it doesn't matter.
  5. A short trial override (for example ending in 5 days) skips trial-7. Fine, or send trial-7 straight away?
