Guest mails: copy review

Nine transactional mails to guests about their own spot on a list: six from Gastcommunicatie F (z8uq9m2vpy) and three request status mails from Requests E (z8uq9m2vga, starting from the v1 comment). No marketing. Copy only: nothing here is built yet. The recommended variant has a lavender border.

Every variant passed the humanizer check (no em dashes, no AI vocabulary). Voice: tone-of-voice.md, method: docs/copy-prompt.md (A plain, B house voice, C different structure).

How to choose

Reply with one letter per id, for example guest-on-list: A, request-declined: B. Mix freely. No reply on an id means the recommended pick.

Shared rules in every guest mail

  • From "{company} via PlusOne" <noreply@plus-one.io>, reply-to the company contact email. The staff member who added the guest is never named. English only.
  • Every footer: why they got it, "Reply to reach {company}", support@plus-one.io, and "Don't want updates from {company}? Unsubscribe". Mails about a live spot add "You stay on the list."; canceled, removed, and declined mails don't, because there's no spot left.
  • Entry is by name at the door. No QR codes promised, and no variant says entry is free (paid tiers exist).
  • Numbers: {people} = 1 + {plus_ones}. The renderer writes "1 person", and with no plus-ones the last details line reads "1 person" instead of "You +0 · 1 people". {requested_people} is what the requester asked for.
  • Gray blocks are conditional: the details block (event, date, doors, location, people), the team's note ({remark}, mandatory on removed, partly approved, and declined), and house rules ({house_rules}, only when the event has them).
  • Limit: 90 words of body. The count includes the details block.
