Guest mails: final copy v3

Final copy after Max's picks: one version per mail, every piece decided. Dashed boxes are the dynamic forms of one line, rendered for 0, 1 and n plus-ones (request-partly: 1, 2 and 4 approved out of 5 asked). Copy only, nothing built yet.

Every variant passed the humanizer check (no em dashes, no AI vocabulary). Voice: tone-of-voice.md.

Decisions (Max, 2026-10-09)

  1. Final picks: guest-on-list A, guest-plus-ones A, guest-event-changed A, guest-event-canceled A, guest-removed A, guest-reminder A (short), request-approved A, request-partly B, request-declined B. Pieces: price line A ("Entry per person"), status link B ("Check your status"), footer A.
  2. No company name in the subject or body. The event name is what guests recognise. The company appears only in the footer.
  3. Every mail shows {event_date} and {event_time}, plus {location} where a spot exists.
  4. Every mail with a spot shows the tier ({tier_name}). A split over tiers lists each tier with its count.
  5. A tier with a door price adds "Entry per person: {tier_price}, pay at the door". No amount is ever written into the copy.
  6. Every mail with a spot has a "Check your status" button to {status_url}, only when the status page exists.
  7. "Add to calendar" (.ics attachment plus a Google Calendar link) in on-list, plus-ones, event-changed, reminder, approved, and partly.
  8. Footer: reason line, "Questions? Mail {company} at {contact_email}, or reply to this email.", the unsubscribe line, and "Trouble with this email? support@plus-one.io" last.
  9. request-declined names {contact_email} in the body.
  10. On the request path only one mail goes out: request-approved or request-partly replaces guest-on-list.
  11. Sender under evaluation: "{event_name} via PlusOne" <noreply@plus-one.io>, reply-to {contact_email}.
  12. Spelling: "canceled".
  13. Every +N and people count is dynamic. Each mail shows its three rendered forms side by side (dashed boxes):
    • 0 plus-ones: never "+0". The sentence drops the plus-ones part ("You're still on the list.") or says "without a plus-one"; the tier line reads "{tier_name} · 1 person"; the price line reads "Entry:" instead of "Entry per person:".
    • 1 plus-one: "+1", "2 people". Removed: "Your +1 is off the list too."
    • n plus-ones (2 or more): "+n" and the total in people (+3 means "4 people"). Removed: "Your +n are off the list too."
    • Singular and plural everywhere: "1 person", otherwise "people". That includes request-partly's approved total and each per-tier count ("Backstage: 1 person", "Guest: 3 people").

Reading the cards

  • Gray block = event details. Small-caps tags mark conditional lines (price, house rules, the team's note, the status button).
  • "Note from the team" keeps the company name out of the body; the team's own words ({remark}) can say anything.
  • The sender is noreply@, but replies reach {contact_email} through reply-to.
