Guest mails: copy review v2

v2 after Max's picks. Part 1 shows the chosen variant of each mail, revised to the rules below. Part 2 has three variants for each new piece: the reminder, the price line, the status link, and the footer. Every chosen mail renders with the recommended piece; your pick of a piece swaps in everywhere. Copy only, nothing built yet.

Every variant passed the humanizer check (no em dashes, no AI vocabulary). Voice: tone-of-voice.md.

How to choose

Reply with one letter per new piece, for example guest-reminder: A, price-line: B, status-link: A, footer: C. Edits to a chosen mail: name the id and the line.

Decisions (Max, 2026-10-09)

  1. Picks: guest-on-list A, guest-plus-ones A, guest-event-changed A, guest-event-canceled A, guest-removed A, request-approved A, request-partly B, request-declined B. guest-reminder is rewritten as a very short mail (3 variants below).
  2. No company name in the subject or body. The event name is what guests recognise. The company appears only in the footer.
  3. Every mail shows {event_date} and {event_time}, plus {location} where a spot exists.
  4. Every mail with a spot shows the tier ({tier_name}). A split over tiers lists each tier with its count.
  5. A tier with a door price adds a price line with {tier_price} (conditional, 3 variants below). No amount is ever written into the copy.
  6. Every mail with a spot links to the guest's status page ({status_url}, conditional on the page existing, 3 variants below).
  7. "Add to calendar" (.ics attachment plus a Google Calendar link) in on-list, plus-ones, event-changed, approved, partly, and reminder.
  8. The footer names the company's contact address explicitly; the unsubscribe line stays; support@plus-one.io only as "Trouble with this email?" (3 variants below).
  9. request-declined: the reply line names {contact_email}.
  10. On the request path only one mail goes out: request-approved or request-partly replaces guest-on-list.
  11. Sender under evaluation: "{event_name} via PlusOne" <noreply@plus-one.io>, reply-to {contact_email}.
  12. Spelling: "canceled".

Reading the cards

  • Gray block = event details. Lines tagged in small caps are conditional (price, per-tier lines, house rules, the team's note, the status link).
  • {people} = 1 + {plus_ones}; the renderer writes "1 person", and with no plus-ones the tier line reads "{tier_name} · 1 person".
  • "Note from the team" keeps the company name out of the body. The team's own words ({remark}) can still name anything they like.
  • The sender is noreply@ but replies reach {contact_email} through reply-to. If "{event_name} via PlusOne" is dropped, the from-line goes back to "{company} via PlusOne" and nothing in the body changes.
