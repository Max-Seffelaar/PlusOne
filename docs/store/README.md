# Store listing drafts (Fase 17 S2, M4)

Drafts for the Play Store and App Store listings, in Dutch (primary market) and English. Content is checked against what the app actually does today — no ticketing, no outbound invites, no in-app billing on the native shell (CLAUDE.md, decisions #36/#32).

- `play-store-listing.md` — Google Play Console fields.
- `app-store-listing.md` — App Store Connect fields.
- `screenshots.md` — shot-list for Max to capture after T1 (tablet layouts) ships.
- `play-review-notes.md` — Play **App access** (review login) text + every other App content declaration (ads, advertising ID, content rating, target audience, news/government/financial/health, permissions). S5.
- `play-rollout.md` — staged rollout internal → (closed) → 10% → 50% → 100%, go/no-go per step, how to halt. S5.
- `play-console-checklist.md` — click-by-click Play Console steps for the first Android submission, in order. Start here. S5.

These are drafts for Max to review and paste into the consoles at submission time (S5) — nothing here is wired into the build.
