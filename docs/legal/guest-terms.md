## 1. PlusOne is the Venue's tool, not a party to your visit

The venue, club or organizer that shared the link with you (the "**Venue**") runs the event and manages its guest list. PlusOne only provides the software the Venue uses to do that. PlusOne is **not a party** to anything between you and the Venue, does not organize the event, and does not decide who gets in.

## 2. A spot on the list is not a guarantee of entry

Requesting a spot, or being placed on a guest list, is a note in the Venue's administration. It is **not a ticket** and gives no right of entry against PlusOne or the Venue. The Venue decides on its own door policy, including admission, refusal, age and ID checks, dress code, how many companions ("+N") it accepts, and any conditions such as a minimum spend. A Venue may approve fewer companions than you asked for, or decline your request without giving a reason to you. Questions about a decision go to the Venue.

## 3. Requesting a spot

**You accept these Guest Terms by sending the request form.** The request page says so right above the send button and links to these Guest Terms and to the Venue's privacy notice, so you can read both before you send. To request a spot you give your name, e-mail address and phone number, so the Venue can reach you; anything else on the form is optional. You must give correct details about yourself, submit only for yourself and the companions you name, and not use automated tools. The Venue approves or declines your request; PlusOne never does. Your status page link is personal: anyone who has it can see the status of your request, so do not share it. PlusOne does not sell tickets and never asks you for payment; if anyone asks you to pay "PlusOne", it is not us.

## 4. Your personal data

The Venue is the **data controller** for the details you submit or that it enters about you; PlusOne processes them only on the Venue's behalf. Your details are visible to the Venue's staff who manage the list and the door. They are **anonymized automatically** after the period chosen by the Venue, at most 60 months after the event, and the Venue can erase you earlier on request. To access, correct or delete your data, or to ask what the Venue does with it, **contact the Venue**. PlusOne never contacts you for marketing; if you ticked "keep me posted", that permission is given to the Venue, and you can withdraw it with the Venue. How PlusOne handles data as a company is in our Privacy Policy: `https://plus-one.io/legal#privacy`.
